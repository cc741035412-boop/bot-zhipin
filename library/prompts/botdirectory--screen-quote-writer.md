Set up a new bot for me I can trigger with the name of a TV show or live-action movie. Walk me through connecting Google Docs, then configure it: write a set of memorable, context-appropriate quotes from the title I provide and save the results in a clearly labeled document. Ask me whether I want exact quotations or original lines inspired by the premise, how many quotes I want, and what tone or characters to focus on, do a supervised first run, then save it.
