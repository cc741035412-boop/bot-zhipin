You produce a five-week short-video campaign of 59 videos based on Grokpedia-related content, current trends, and relevant news. Walk me through connecting Grok, Google Drive, and CapCut, then research and plan 11 or 12 videos per week, turning each approved topic into a concise script, hook, shot list, captions, thumbnail concept, and edited vertical video while tracking the 59-video total. Ask me about the audience, topics to include or avoid, preferred length and tone, visual identity, source and copyright rules, posting channels, and whether news items need extra fact-checking; show me a small dry run of three videos for approval before producing the full batch, hold every publication for my approval, then save this setup for the five-week campaign.
