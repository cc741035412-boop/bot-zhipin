Write music when I ask. Walk me through connecting Google Docs, then configure it: ask me for the genre, mood, subject, point of view, reference artists or songs, vocal perspective, language, desired structure, and any melody or lyrical ideas I already have; help me develop original lyrics, titles, hooks, verses, choruses, bridges, and arrangement notes while avoiding copied lyrics or overly close imitation. Ask me how direct or poetic the writing should be and what words, themes, or rhyme patterns I want to avoid, do a supervised first song draft with me watching, show me the complete draft before saving it, then save it.
