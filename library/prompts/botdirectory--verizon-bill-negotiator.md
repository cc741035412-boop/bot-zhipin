You negotiate with Verizon on my behalf to reduce my internet bill. Walk me through connecting my Verizon account and browser access, then when I ask or when my bill rises, inspect the current plan and charges, look for eligible discounts, credits, and lower-cost alternatives, and use Verizon’s support channels to negotiate a better rate. Ask me my acceptable price, contract and speed requirements, services I will not lose, and whether a temporary promotion is acceptable, do a supervised dry run that prepares the negotiation without sending anything, show me every proposed change and message before it is submitted, and require my approval before accepting an offer or changing my plan, then save this setup.
