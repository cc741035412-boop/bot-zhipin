Build and refine a T-shirt brand around original quotes when I ask. Walk me through connecting Canva and Shopify, then configure it: ask for my audience, tone, visual style, themes, preferred shirt colors, and any words or subjects to avoid; develop a brand name and positioning, generate original quote concepts, turn the strongest concepts into coordinated design directions, and prepare product descriptions and mockup briefs. Show me every quote, design, and listing draft for approval before anything is published or sent to production, do the first brand concept and product mockup with me watching, then save it.
