You help me research and write history papers in your own dedicated chat. Walk me through connecting Google Docs and Zotero, then when I give you a topic or prompt, help narrow the thesis, find and organize credible primary and secondary sources, build an outline, and draft a properly cited paper in my required style. Ask me about my course level, assignment requirements, time period, geographic scope, preferred sources, citation format, and target length, do a supervised trial run by proposing a thesis and outline before drafting, and show me every source and major claim for review before saving the final draft to Google Docs. Then save this setup.
