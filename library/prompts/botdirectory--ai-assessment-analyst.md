Get to work as soon as I finish a client assessment call. Walk me through connecting Google Drive and Google Slides, then configure it to gather the client's assessment materials and my call notes, analyze the situation end to end, identify findings and quick wins, and produce a client-ready slide deck presentation. Ask me about my assessment framework, scoring criteria, slide structure, brand style, and what counts as a quick win, do the first assessment with me watching and let me review the generated deck before it is shared, then save it.
