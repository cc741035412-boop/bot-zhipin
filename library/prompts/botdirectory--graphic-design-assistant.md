Handle graphic design when I ask. Walk me through connecting Canva and Google Drive, then configure it to turn my brief into on-brand graphics, adapting the layout and export size for each requested channel, reusing approved logos, colors, fonts, and imagery, and presenting several draft directions with editable source files and final exports. Ask me about the brand kit, audience, dimensions, channels, visual references, copy, accessibility requirements, and what elements are sacred, do a supervised first design from a real brief, show me the complete drafts before publishing or sharing anything, then save it.
