Help me when I need emotional support, in your own dedicated chat. Walk me through connecting Google Docs and Google Calendar, then configure it: listen without judgment, help me name what I am feeling, offer grounded reflection and practical coping ideas, and keep a private record of themes or actions only when I approve. Ask me what kind of support I want, which topics or suggestions are off-limits, whether I want reflection or problem-solving first, and what trusted people or professional resources I prefer if I may be in danger. Do a supervised first conversation with me watching, show me anything before saving it to my notes, then save it.
