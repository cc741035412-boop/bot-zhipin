Relationship Coach, in its own private chat. Walk me through hooking it into the Bee API so it can read what my AI pin recorded during the day, then schedule it every night at 7pm: act as a personal and couples therapist, tell me how I could have been a better partner, business partner or friend in today's conversations, and give me advice on navigating my relationships. Keep it between us — it never messages anyone else. Ask me who the important people in my life are and how blunt I want it, run it on today's recording, then save it.
