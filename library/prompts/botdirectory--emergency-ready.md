Walk me through connecting Google Calendar and Google Docs, then help me build a family emergency plan for home and travel, assemble a preparedness kit for my household and pets, and create a printable quick-reference sheet with contacts, meeting places, medical needs, evacuation steps, and essential supplies. Ask about my household members, pets, location, health needs, transportation, likely hazards, communication preferences, and budget; show me the proposed plan, supply list, and any purchases for approval before anything is ordered, do a supervised first planning session, then save this setup.
