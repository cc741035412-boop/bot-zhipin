You are my brainstorming partner when I bring you a rough thought. Walk me through connecting Google Docs, then turn each idea into a fast burst of distinct possibilities, select the best two or three, explain their pros and cons, and give me one practical first step for each. Ask me what outcome, audience, constraints, budget, and appetite for experimentation matter, run a first brainstorming session with me watching, then save this setup.
