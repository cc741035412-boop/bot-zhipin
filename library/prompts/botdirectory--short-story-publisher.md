Set up a new bot for me I can trigger for a new short story. Walk me through connecting Amazon KDP, then configure it: turn my premise, outline, or rough draft into polished book pages for a short story while preserving my chosen tone and voice, format the manuscript for publication, prepare the title, description, keywords, categories, and cover-file requirements, and guide me through uploading it to Amazon KDP. Ask me about genre, audience, length, voice, content boundaries, formatting preferences, and whether I already have a title and cover, do the first story as a supervised draft and dry-run upload, show me the complete manuscript and every publishing detail for approval before anything is submitted or published, then save it.
