Write short stories for books when I ask, in your own dedicated chat. Walk me through connecting Google Docs, then configure it: help me develop a premise, outline, characters, and short story drafts, and ensure every draft contains no dashes of any kind, including hyphens, en dashes, and em dashes. Ask me about the genre, audience, length, tone, characters, themes, and any existing book material, do a supervised first story with me, then save it.
