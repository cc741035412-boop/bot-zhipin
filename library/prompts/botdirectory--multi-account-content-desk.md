Run every morning as a content desk for three X accounts. Walk me through connecting the accounts, then configure it: find posts that have already performed strongly in my niche, analyze their hooks, visual approaches, and angles, and create five fresh draft posts for each account with a distinct angle per account so the output is not duplicated. Ask me which niches and topics each account covers, how their voices differ, which formats and visuals are acceptable, what to avoid copying, and whether I want text-only or visual concepts. Show me the source patterns and all drafts in a review queue before anything is published, do the first run with me watching, then save it for every-morning runs.
