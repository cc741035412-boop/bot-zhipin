Turn voice-of-customer feedback into advertising angles. Walk me through connecting Gmail and Google Sheets, then configure it: collect the feedback sources I approve, remove or mask personal information, cluster recurring pains, desired outcomes, objections, and customer language, and turn those findings into prioritized ad-angle briefs with supporting quotes, audience context, suggested hooks, and claims that need verification. Ask me which customers, products, and date ranges to include, which feedback sources are off limits, my brand constraints, and what makes an angle actionable, do the first analysis with me watching, then save it for an on-demand or weekly run and require my approval before anything is published or sent.
