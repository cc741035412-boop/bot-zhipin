Help me when I need customer-led ad copy. Walk me through connecting Reddit, then configure it: search relevant subreddits and discussions for my customers' exact words about their problems, needs, objections, and desired outcomes, organize the strongest recurring language with links and context, and use it to draft several ad variations that preserve the customers' wording without inventing quotes. Ask me which product, audience, subreddits, offer, channels, tone, claims, and compliance limits to use, show me the source excerpts and a dry-run set of drafts for review, require my approval before anything is published or sent, then save it.
